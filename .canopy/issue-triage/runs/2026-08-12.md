## Holistic scan (operator-requested, mid-run)

The operator approved implementing #796 and #856 but asked first for *"a full
scan of everything open to see if other issues have been detected or give a
more wholistic view of the challenge."* Read-only pass over all 75 remaining
open issues, then full reads of #1094, #873, #1069, #869, #1191.

It changed both items. Neither is standalone.

### Cluster: which-home-am-i-on — #796, #869, #1191 (+ #863 shipped, #893 closed)

`deliver-home-daily-visits` should be the canonical Deliver-home signal
everywhere. Worth a static invariant that no recipe uses
`deliver-home-job-card`/viewJobCard alone as a Learn/Deliver discriminator —
because **#1191's proposed `deliver-home-reentry.yaml` keys its home anchor on
exactly that selector.** If it ships as written it inherits the bug #863 just
fixed. Flagged on PR #1207.

### Cluster: proceed-with-warn-is-load-bearing — #856, #1094, #1069 (constrained by #873)

- **#856** — deck level: a hollow deck ships as `proceed-with-warn`.
- **#1094** — review-offering level: a run reached "ready for Sophie" with the
  Deliver app never device-walked. Three turn sessions drafted the reply and
  none of their gates caught it; the operator did. This is #856 generalized.
- **#1069** — vocabulary level: `partial` is not in `PHASE_STATUSES`, so a
  *supported* mode has no legal terminal status and the orchestrator
  retry-loops on a phase that is genuinely finished.

**#873 is the blocking constraint, and it is why #856 must not be implemented
standalone.** The PersonalID sign-up completion half (OTP → photo →
permissions → done) is *uncapturable* with the demo test user: `+7426` phones
bypass SMS OTP server-side, and the pre-existing account turns registration
into account-recovery, which returns the stored photo, skips the permissions
dialog, and renders "Account Recovered" instead of "Profile complete!". All
five aliases are `placeholder: true` in the **shared** common pool, so the
entire second half of the sign-up walkthrough is text-only in **every** opp
deck, permanently and by design.

A naive visual-coverage floor therefore fires on 100% of runs. The gate has to
whitelist pool-level placeholders and measure only *opp-specific* capture — and
which way to resolve that is a decision #873 explicitly leaves open ("Fix
options (pick one; needs a decision)"). That is a human product call, not
something to infer inside a triage.
