## The three judgment calls worth re-reading

**#869 had a third door the issue never considered.** The body offered "drop
the end-of-palette shot (or guard it on fresh-install)", and the scoping
comment correctly proved the unguarded form loses all coverage on the
fresh-install path. That left a device-validated guard as the apparent only
option — hence `blocked-on-validation` for three weeks. But a guard was never
viable: both home anchors are asserted visible at end-of-palette so no
predicate separates the paths, and an `evalScript` flag doesn't survive
`captureAllBoundaries` chunking. Dropping the *nested* shot instead removes
the duplicate with fresh-install output byte-for-byte unchanged. The new
invariant pins both halves at once — exactly one Deliver-home shot, at column
0 — and was verified as a negative control against the pre-fix palettes.

**#856's blocker was in the proposed mechanism, not the problem.** Yesterday's
holistic scan concluded #856 could not ship without the #873 product decision,
because a coverage gate would need to read `placeholder: true` out of the
Drive-only pool manifest. It doesn't: the spec already partitioned
`manifest.common` from `manifest.opp`, and every uncapturable alias sits on
the non-opp side. Scoping the metric to `manifest.opp` excludes them by
construction. That reversal is a design change, not new information.

**#873's decision came from the operator and is better than any of the three
options in the issue.** Not "capture with a real-OTP identity", not "commit
fixtures awaiting capture", not "drop the aliases" — but *make them part of
the template*. That ends the perpetual-miss loop the fixtures framing created,
and it is what lets #856's gate exist at all.

## Meta

CI gotcha worth knowing: **GitHub does not run required checks on a
conflicting PR**, and `gh pr checks` reports *"no checks reported on the
'<branch>' branch"* — which reads exactly like a CI misconfiguration. If
`mergeStateStatus` is `DIRTY`, rebase; the checks appear on the next push.
Main bumped five times during this run (0.13.768 → 0.13.774), so every PR
after the first needed `version-bump.sh --rebase-first`.
