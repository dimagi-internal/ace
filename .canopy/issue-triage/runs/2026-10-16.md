## Outcome (scope expanded by the operator to a full burn-down)

The operator asked to "burn this entire list down", so the contract became **full sweep**. Final state: **122 open issues → 25**; 3 open PRs → #3063 pending (re-landed #2796).

Strategy and what it taught:
- **Oldest-first cleanup pass** over the 89 untriaged issues: 20 closed as already fixed, obsolete or wrong-repo (a 22% close rate, vs 6% on the 33 newest). The other ~60 were real.
- **Fix agents in parallel, one PR each, choked the repo.** Every PR edits VERSION, so each merge forced every other PR to rebase. ~25 PRs rebasing in parallel saturated the Actions runners and exhausted GraphQL (land-pr polling). It also exposed #3095: version-bump's open-PR claim scan failed open under the rate limit, and 8 PRs claimed 0.13.1959. Throughput was ~2 merges/hour.
- **Merge trains fixed it.** Each agent's diff was applied (version files excluded) onto main in one branch, with one local full-suite run and one bump. Train 1 #3109: 27 PRs, 46 issues. Train 2 #3111: 7 branches, 26 issues. Round-3 agents worked in "train mode": they pushed a branch with no PR and no bump.
- Combining surfaced rail interactions none of the source PRs hit alone: the rail registry, the negative-control grounding ratchet, and the ace#1964 env-loading rail. Fixed in train commits.
